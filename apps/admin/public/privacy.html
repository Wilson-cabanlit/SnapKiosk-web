SNAPKIOSK STUDIO

Privacy notice

SnapKiosk is used by photo booth studios to manage rentals, events, kiosks, and private photo galleries. The studio operating this service is responsible for responding to privacy requests.

Information we process

Depending on the studio’s event setup, SnapKiosk may process account and client contact details, booking and event information, kiosk diagnostics, photo files, consent records, gallery access records, and audit activity.

Photos, consent, and sharing

Photos are stored in private object storage. A guest link provides access only to the associated gallery while that link is valid. A client album is a separate access path and is included only where the studio has enabled it and recorded client gallery consent. Photo files are not used for facial recognition or advertising analytics.

Retention and deletion

Guest access, client album access, and photo retention are controlled by event settings. The service removes photo objects through scheduled cleanup after authorized access and retention periods end. Contact the studio owner to request access, correction, or deletion. The owner can review deletion requests from the admin workspace.

Security

Administrator access uses password-protected accounts and expiring sessions. Photo objects remain private; access is checked by the API. Production deployments should use HTTPS and restrict administrator access to approved origins.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the photo booth studio that provided your event or gallery link. The studio owner’s contact details should be provided with your event booking or gallery invitation.
